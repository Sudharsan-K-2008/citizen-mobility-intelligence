How MADRITHM works

  1. Collect – citizens and permitted public sources submit reports.
  2. Clean and filter – personal data is removed; non-transport text is filtered out.
  3. AI/NLP analysis – category, issue, affected users, language (English/Tamil/Tanglish).
  4. Location and time – places are matched to map coordinates. Unknown places are flagged, never invented.
  5. Severity and sentiment – scored separately, with the reasons shown.
  6. Duplicate grouping – similar reports in the same place and time become one possible incident. Copy-pasted posts do not raise confidence.
  7. Dashboard – map, analytics and filters. Officials verify, resolve or reject incidents.

Data transparency: reports come from citizen submissions, administrator CSV imports and clearly labelled sample/demo data. Gemini interprets submitted text; it does not itself provide live traffic data. No automatic Google Maps, social-media, CCTV or government-feed ingestion is connected in this prototype.

Important: severity is an estimate and reports are unverified until an official confirms them. This tool supports decisions; it does not replace official verification.
